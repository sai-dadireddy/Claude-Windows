PURPOSE
Validate Benefit Election History and Benefit Coverage History for the newly
hired employee after benefit elections are submitted. This step confirms that
elections are properly recorded in the system.

PREREQUISITES
- BEN-4-0010-01 (Hire Employee) completed successfully
- BEN-4-0010-02 (Benefit Elections) completed successfully
- Employee has submitted benefit elections
- User has Benefits Administrator security role

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMATED STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. enter search box as "{TEST_EMPLOYEE_NAME}"
2. wait for search results (2 seconds)
3. click employee result from search
4. wait for worker profile to load (3 seconds)
5. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-03_step05_worker_profile.png"
6. click tab "Benefits" or navigate to Benefits section
7. wait for page load (2 seconds)
8. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-03_step08_benefits_tab.png"
9. click link "Benefit Elections"
10. wait for Benefit Elections page to load (3 seconds)
11. verify current elections display Medical plan
12. verify current elections display Dental plan
13. verify current elections display Vision plan
14. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-03_step14_elections.png"
15. click link "View Election History" or "Benefit Election History"
16. wait for history page to load (3 seconds)
17. verify election history shows New Hire event
18. verify election effective date matches hire date
19. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-03_step19_election_history.png"
20. navigate back to Benefits section
21. click link "Benefit Coverage" or "Coverage History"
22. wait for coverage page to load (3 seconds)
23. verify coverage start date matches effective date
24. verify coverage status shows "Active"
25. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-03_step25_coverage_history.png"
26. verify all elected plans show in coverage
