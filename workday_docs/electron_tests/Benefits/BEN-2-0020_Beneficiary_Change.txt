TASK/STEP: Change Beneficiaries

PREREQUISITES:
- User logged in with Benefits Administrator or worker self-service
- Test data: Worker with life insurance or retirement plan

ELECTRON STEPS:
1. enter search box as "Change Beneficiaries"
2. wait for search results
3. click search result containing "Change Beneficiaries"
4. wait for page to load
5. enter worker field as "{WORKER_ID}"
6. screenshot as "BEN-2-0020_page.png"
7. select benefit plan requiring beneficiary
8. add or update beneficiary information
9. enter allocation percentage (must equal 100%)
10. click button "Submit"
11. wait for confirmation
12. screenshot as "BEN-2-0020_complete.png"

VERIFICATION:
- [ ] Beneficiary Change task completed successfully
- [ ] Beneficiary designation updated
- [ ] Allocation percentages total 100%

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Step 1-2 fails: Verify search functionality is working; try refreshing page
- Step 3 fails: Check if user has appropriate security role
- Step 5 fails: Verify worker has life insurance or retirement enrollment
- Step 9 fails: Percentages must total exactly 100%
- Step 10 fails: Required fields may be missing (SSN, DOB, Address)
- General failure: Verify beneficiary designation is enabled for plan

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
