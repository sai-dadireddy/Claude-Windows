PURPOSE
Verify the Benefit Election step during onboarding for a newly hired employee.
This step ensures the employee can enroll in benefit plans as part of the
New Hire to Pay with Elections business process.

PREREQUISITES
- BEN-4-0010-01 (Hire Employee) completed successfully
- Employee has active hire event with benefits eligibility
- Benefit plans configured for new hire enrollment
- User has Benefits Administrator security role

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. wait for search results (2 seconds)
3. click search result "Change Benefits" with type "Task"
4. wait for dialog to load (3 seconds)
5. enter "Employee" as "{TEST_EMPLOYEE_ID}"
6. wait for employee search (2 seconds)
7. click employee result from dropdown
8. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-02_step08_employee_selected.png"
9. select "Benefit Event Type" as "New Hire"
10. enter "Effective Date" as "{TEST_EFFECTIVE_DATE}"

13. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-02_step13_enrollment_page.png"
