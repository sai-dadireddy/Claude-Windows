PURPOSE
Validate Payroll reflects the benefit elections made during onboarding. This step
confirms that benefit deductions are correctly calculated and will appear on the
employee's paycheck.

PREREQUISITES
- BEN-4-0010-01 through 03 completed successfully
- Employee has active benefit elections
- Payroll is configured for benefit deductions
- User has Payroll Administrator or View Payroll security role

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMATED STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. enter search box as "{TEST_EMPLOYEE_NAME}"
2. wait for search results (2 seconds)
3. click employee result from search
4. wait for worker profile to load (3 seconds)
5. click tab "Pay" or navigate to Pay section
6. wait for page load (2 seconds)
7. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-04_step07_pay_tab.png"
8. click link "Payroll" or "View Payroll Results"
9. wait for payroll page to load (3 seconds)
10. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-04_step10_payroll_page.png"
11. verify section "Deductions" displays
12. verify Medical deduction appears with correct amount
13. verify Dental deduction appears with correct amount
14. verify Vision deduction appears with correct amount
15. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-04_step15_deductions.png"
16. click to view deduction details if available
17. verify deduction frequency matches pay period
18. verify deduction effective date aligns with coverage start
19. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-04_step19_deduction_details.png"
20. navigate to "Pay Statement" or run payroll preview
21. wait for statement to load (3 seconds)
22. verify benefit deductions appear on pay statement
23. verify net pay calculation includes deductions
24. screenshot as "BEN-4-0010-04_step24_pay_statement.png"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFICATION

- [ ] Pay tab/section accessible for employee

- [ ] Medical, Dental, Vision deduction amounts are correct
- [ ] Deduction effective dates match coverage start
